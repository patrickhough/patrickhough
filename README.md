# Patrick Hough

Postdoctoral researcher in cryptography at the Privacy and Cryptography (PACY) group, Research Institute CODE, Universität der Bundeswehr München.

My research focuses on post-quantum cryptography, particularly lattice-based cryptography, threshold signatures and distributed key generation, zero-knowledge proofs, and privacy-preserving protocols.
